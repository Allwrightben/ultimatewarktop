About Ultimate Worktop

The Ultimate Worktop supplies quartz, granite, marble, porcelain, and Dekton worktops for residential properties and multi-plot developments. We understand how the building trade works and focus on keeping projects on track, meeting deadlines, and helping clients stay within budget. From material supply to templating and installation, I provide a straightforward and reliable service for builders, developers, and homeowners.

Every project is managed directly by the business owner, from first enquiry through to installation. That means one contact, clear updates and a better experience for builders, developers and busy homeowners.

Why clients choose us

  • Responsiveness on multi-kitchen and multi-plot projects
  • Fair, competitive pricing and volume discounts
  • No middlemen, direct communication
  • Reliable deadlines and smooth coordination
