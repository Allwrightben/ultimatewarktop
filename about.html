About Ultimate Worktop

The Ultimate Worktop supplies and installs quartz, granite, marble, porcelain, and Dekton worktops for residential properties and multi-plot developments.


We understand the demands of the building industry and focus on keeping projects on track, meeting deadlines, and helping clients stay within budget. From material supply and templating through to installation, we provide a straightforward, reliable service for builders, developers, and homeowners.


Every project is managed directly by the business owner, from initial enquiry through to installation. This means a single point of contact, clear communication, and a seamless experience throughout the process.

Why clients choose us

  • Trusted for single kitchens and large scale developments
  • Competitive pricing and volume discounts
  • Direct owner led service
  • Reliable lead times and seamless project delivery
