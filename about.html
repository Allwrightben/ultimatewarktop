About The Ultimate Worktop

The Ultimate Worktop supplies and installs premium quartz, granite, marble, porcelain and Dekton worktops for residential, commercial and multi-plot developments across London and the South East.

We work closely with builders, developers and homeowners, providing competitive pricing, reliable lead times and clear communication to help keep every project on track. Whether it's a single kitchen or a large-scale development, our focus is on delivering a professional, dependable service from start to finish.

Every project is managed directly by the business owner from initial enquiry through to installation. With one dedicated point of contact throughout, you'll always know who you're dealing with, ensuring clear communication and complete accountability at every stage.
