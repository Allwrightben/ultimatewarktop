The Complete Service

Measure Twice Install Once

Over 15 years of industry experience, from templating and fabrication through to final installation. Attention to detail at every stage helps ensure a precise fit and deliver an overall premium service.

1. Material selection

Wide range of quartz, granite, marble, porcelain and Dekton surfaces, with access to leading suppliers and manufacturers.

2. On-site templating

Accurate measurements ensure every worktop is manufactured to the correct dimensions before fabrication begins.

3. Fabrication

Worktops are cut, shaped and finished to specification, including sink and hob cut-outs, profile edges, drainer grooves, upstands, splashbacks and bespoke details where required.

4. Installation

Carefully installed with attention to detail, ensuring a precise fit and a clean finish.
