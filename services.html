Services built for builders, developers and kitchen fitters.

From worktop supply to templating and installation, we support multi-plot projects with consistent delivery, clear communication and trade-focused pricing.

What we offer

1. Material selection

Choose from quartz, granite, marble, porcelain and Dekton. We help you balance style, durability and pricing for each project.

2. On-site templating

Precise templates reduce waste and hold the project timeline, especially where several kitchens are being fitted together.

3. Installation

Experienced installers deliver a clean finish that keeps your build schedule moving and your customers happy.

How the process works

Step 1:

Send project details and plans using the enquiry form, email or phone. Include kitchens, materials and timescales.

Step 2:

Receive a tailored quote with trade pricing options and suggested materials based on the project scope.

Step 3:

We supply materials, template on site and install to keep the build on schedule, with one person managing the whole job.

Who this works best for

Our service is ideal for builders, developers, kitchen fitters and contractors who need dependable worktops across multiple kitchens and developments.
